
# CloudPad
A simple nine-key macropad that can open up my favorite and most-used apps

**Bill of Materials**
- Seeed XIAO RP2040
- 9 through-hole 1N4148 diodes
- 9 MX-style switches
- 9 white blank DSA keycaps
- 1 EC11 rotary encoder
